🤖 Activity 4: AI ENERGY EXPERT CHALLENGE

Can you use AI like an Energy Expert?

AI can help us learn, but AI can sometimes make mistakes.
Your job is to ask, check and think!

STEP 1 — ASK AI 💬

Copy this question into an AI assistant:

"Explain what happens to energy when I switch on a torch. Explain it in a way that a Grade 4 learner can understand."

STEP 2 — READ 📖

Read the AI's answer carefully.

STEP 3 — CHECK 🔎

Compare the AI's answer with your lesson notes.

Is the AI's answer correct?

Explain your answer.

STEP 4 — LET US TRY OUR OWN QUESTION 💡

Use an AI assistant to explore this question:

"Give me three examples of energy being used at school, and explain the type of energy they release."
